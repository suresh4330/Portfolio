## Overview

**IP-SAKTI Sahayak** is a multilingual AI assistant engineered for the **Smart India Hackathon (SIH26045)** capstone, delivering cited legal and regulatory guidance on patentability and biodiversity compliance.

## Engineering Contribution & Architecture

- **Backend & Data Integration (Module 04 Lead - Suresh)**: Developed asynchronous FastAPI endpoints, PostgreSQL relational schemas, authentication, and audit event logs.
- **Source-Cited RAG**: Enforces constrained answer synthesis where every claim must resolve to an authoritative citation ID.
- **Uncertainty & Referral**: Features safe abstention and structured referral packets when evidence is inconclusive.
